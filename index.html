What changed

New words are no longer thrown straight into the full-deck quiz. Learn mode introduces them first. Review still uses expanding intervals: roughly 5 minutes → 20 minutes → 1 day → 3 days → 7 days → 14 days → 30 days → 90 days.

Definitions, examples, pronunciation metadata, and etymology are bundled into the site as static data. Study sessions do not depend on external APIs.
Source deck derived from the supplied TakeTest HAR capture. Lexical enrichment is prebuilt from Wiktionary-derived Kaikki/Wiktextract data and stored locally with the site.
